Made for the table

Play together, nearby

📶 No Wi-Fi network needed

Phones connect directly to each other. Keep Wi-Fi switched on and stay close, even in airplane mode.

🚪 Open or private rooms

Open rooms show up for everyone nearby. Private rooms are hidden and need a 6-character code.

🤖 Friendly bots

Practise any game on your own: Easy, Normal or Hard. Bots can fill empty seats too.

🎉 Cheer each other on

Reactions, quick text chat and hold-to-talk voice clips, only to the people in your room.

🙈 Hide your hand

One tap covers your cards; touch to peek when someone's looking over your shoulder.

🏆 A review every round

Everyone stays on the final table: the winning hand, everyone's cards and points.
