SeatChatter Support

SeatChatter lets you play card and board games with the people around you: Last Card, Big Two, Fight the Landlord, Chess, Chinese Chess and Cake Caper. Phones connect directly to each other. You don't need an account, a Wi-Fi network or the internet.

Contact us: androidoven@gmail.com
We reply as soon as we can, and we review safety reports promptly.

Getting started

  1. Host: tap New room, pick a game, then tap Create open room (or make it private).
  2. Everyone else: the room shows up under Parties nearby. Tap it to join, or tap Enter code and type the room code.
  3. Tap I'm ready!. The host taps Let's go!.

Playing alone? Tap Practice with bots to play any game against friendly bots on your phone.

I can't see a room or can't join

  • Turn Wi-Fi on on every phone. You don't have to join a network, and it works in airplane mode as long as Wi-Fi is on.
  • Allow Local Network: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › SeatChatter must be on.
  • Stay close: being in the same room works best.
  • Private rooms don't appear in the list. Ask the host for the 6-character code and use Enter code.
  • Everyone needs the same version of SeatChatter. If you're asked to update, update from the App Store.
  • Still stuck? The host can close the room and create it again.

Voice clips

  • Hold the mic button to record a clip (up to 15 seconds), and let go to send it. Slide up to cancel.
  • Allow the microphone in Settings › Apps › SeatChatter.
  • Can't hear clips? Make sure your phone isn't in silent mode, or tap a clip in the chat to play it.

Safety

  • Mute: tap a player's avatar (or their name in chat), then Mute voice. Their voice clips are hidden completely, including ones already in the chat; their text chat still shows.
  • Block: in the same menu, tap Block. You won't see their chat, hear their voice clips or see their reactions.
  • Report: in the same menu, tap Report…. An email to us opens with the details. To report one voice clip, long-press it in the chat and tap Report…: the clip is attached to the email. Reporting also blocks the player.
  • Remove: if you're the host, you can remove someone from your room.
  • Chat filter: on by default; you can change it in your Profile › Safety. Your blocked list is there too.

Too much motion?

Turn on Calm effects in your profile, or switch on Reduce Motion in iOS Settings › Accessibility.

Rules

Every game has a picture rule book: tap the ? button next to the game.

联系我们：androidoven@gmail.com
我们会尽快回复，并及时处理安全举报。

开始

  1. 房主：点 新建房间，选择游戏，然后点 创建公开房间（也可以设为私密）。
  2. 其他人：房间会出现在 附近的派对 里，点一下就能加入；也可以点 输入房间码，输入房间码加入。
  3. 点 我准备好了！，然后房主开始游戏。

一个人？点 和机器人练习，在自己的手机上和友好的机器人玩任何游戏。

看不到房间或无法加入

  • 打开 Wi-Fi：每台手机都要打开。不需要连接网络，飞行模式下只要 Wi-Fi 开着也可以。
  • 允许本地网络：设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 本地网络 › SeatChatter 需要打开。
  • 靠近一些：在同一个房间里效果最好。
  • 私密房间不会出现在列表里。请向房主要 6 位房间码，然后用 输入房间码 加入。
  • 大家需要使用相同版本的 SeatChatter。如果提示需要更新，请到 App Store 更新。
  • 还是不行？房主可以关闭房间后重新创建。

语音片段

  • 按住麦克风按钮录音（最长 15 秒），松手发送，上滑取消。
  • 允许麦克风：在 设置 › App › SeatChatter 中打开。
  • 听不到语音？请确认手机没有静音，或者点聊天里的语音片段来播放。

安全

  • 静音：点对方的头像（或聊天里的名字），然后点 静音语音。对方的语音片段会被完全隐藏（包括聊天里已有的），文字聊天仍会显示。
  • 屏蔽：在同一菜单里点 屏蔽。你将看不到对方的聊天和表情，也听不到对方的语音片段。
  • 举报：在同一菜单里点 举报…，会打开一封附有详细信息的邮件。要举报某条语音片段，在聊天里长按它，然后点 举报…：这条语音片段会作为附件加入邮件。举报的同时也会屏蔽对方。
  • 移出房间：房主可以把人移出自己的房间。
  • 聊天过滤：默认开启，可以在 个人资料 › 安全 里更改。屏蔽名单也在那里。

动效太多？

在个人资料里打开 安静特效，或在 iOS 设置 › 辅助功能 里打开"减弱动态效果"。

规则

每款游戏都有图解规则：点游戏旁边的 ? 按钮。

隐私

SeatChatter 不收集任何数据。详见隐私政策。
