Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version: we don't collect any data. SeatChatter has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the SeatChatter app for iPhone ("SeatChatter", "we", "us").

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile: the display name and avatar colour you choose.
  • Your settings: for example hints, the chat filter, calm effects and voice auto-play.
  • People you've blocked: their in-app ID and name, so the block keeps working.
  • A room snapshot if you host a room, so it can reopen if the app is closed unexpectedly. It's removed when the room closes.

This information never leaves your phone except as described below. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What you share with people in your room

When you join a room, your phone connects directly to the other phones in it over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or the local network you're on. The people in that room receive:

  • your display name and avatar colour;
  • the chat messages, reactions and voice clips you send;
  • your game moves.

Voice clips are recorded only while you hold the mic button (up to 15 seconds). They're sent to the phones in the room and aren't stored. Chat, reactions and voice clips live only in the app's memory while the room is open, and are gone when you leave.

This traffic goes from phone to phone. It never passes through us, and we can't see it.

Practice mode

Practice with bots runs entirely on your phone, without any networking.

Reports

If you report someone, the app opens an email to our support address in your own mail app. The email includes the room code, the player's name and in-app ID, the reason you chose, their recent chat lines and the app version. You can read and edit it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use reports only to look into safety issues, and we delete them once they're resolved. If you report a voice clip, the clip is attached to your email, so we can hear it. It's attached only in the Mail app's compose screen, which you see before sending; if your mail app can't attach files, the clip isn't included.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find and connect to phones near you.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the mic button, to record a voice clip.

You can change both at any time in the iOS Settings app. SeatChatter doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or Bluetooth.

Children

SeatChatter is not directed at children under 13. It lets people nearby chat with each other; parents can use Screen Time to restrict it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: androidoven@gmail.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日

简而言之：我们不收集任何数据。SeatChatter 没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计分析、没有广告，也没有追踪。你在应用里的任何操作都不会发送给我们。

本政策说明你在使用 iPhone 应用 SeatChatter（"SeatChatter"、"我们"）时，你的信息会如何处理。

保存在你手机上的信息

  • 个人资料：你选择的昵称和头像颜色。
  • 设置：例如提示、聊天过滤、安静特效、语音自动播放。
  • 你屏蔽的人：对方的应用内 ID 和名字，以便屏蔽持续有效。
  • 房间快照：如果你是房主，会保存一份快照，以便应用意外关闭后可以重新打开房间。房间关闭时会删除。

除下文所述外，这些信息不会离开你的手机。删除应用即会删除全部信息。

你与同房间的人分享的内容

加入房间后，你的手机会通过点对点 Wi-Fi 或你所在的局域网，直接连接到同一房间的其他手机。同房间的人会收到：

  • 你的昵称和头像颜色；
  • 你发送的聊天消息、表情和语音片段；
  • 你的游戏操作。

只有在你按住麦克风按钮时才会录制语音片段（最长 15 秒）。语音片段会发送到同房间的手机，不会被保存。聊天、表情和语音片段只在房间开着时保存在应用内存中，离开后即消失。

这些数据只在手机之间直接传输，不会经过我们，我们也无法看到。

练习模式

和机器人练习完全在你的手机上进行，不使用任何网络。

举报

举报某人时，应用会在你自己的邮件应用里打开一封发给我们客服邮箱的邮件。邮件包含房间码、对方的名字和应用内 ID、你选择的原因、对方最近的聊天内容以及应用版本。发送前你可以查看和修改，不点发送就不会发出。我们只用举报来处理安全问题，处理完成后即删除。如果你举报一条语音片段，这条语音片段会作为附件加入你的邮件，方便我们收听。只有在你发送前能看到的「邮件」撰写界面中才会附上；如果你的邮件应用无法添加附件，就不会包含这条语音片段。

权限

  • 本地网络：用于发现并连接你身边的手机。
  • 麦克风：只在你按住麦克风按钮时录制语音片段。

你可以随时在 iOS 设置中更改这两项权限。SeatChatter 不使用你的位置、通讯录、照片、相机或蓝牙。

儿童

SeatChatter 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。应用允许身边的人互相聊天，家长可以用"屏幕使用时间"加以限制。

变更

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面和上方日期。
